# Make the source packet the desk’s starting point

On 2026-11-24 the owner chose a single entry for new studies. This supersedes the dual entry in [the source-led discovery decision](0025-source-led-discovery.md).

The desk opens on mixed file intake, pasted material and the saved source library. The owner can inspect and reorder the packet before asking the lead to read it. Optional context, model settings and the writing sample remain editable. Existing admitted sessions keep their original execution graph and request identity.

The Methodology page exposes the working-sentence decision, the resulting assignment and the paths through parking, editorial review and revision. Its examples are illustrative. Actual decisions and assignments live in each study with their saved requests.
