# Live Research Swarm studies

On 2026-11-25 the owner chose Research Swarm as the Research Desk workflow.
New studies enable web search and expert consultation and use GPT-6.2 Astra as
the report writer. Leads and researchers choose when their evidence is sufficient
for the assigned question. The default leaves study time, lead turns, and analyst
passes uncapped by the setup. Request safeguards and the workspace allowance
still apply. Saved study and Workbench workflows keep their pinned settings.

## Seeing and directing the work

The study groups saved files and model receipts by the agents that produced them.
The team graph follows observed mandates, delegations, consultations, and report
receipts. Both views open the same workspace and exact saved artifacts.

The owner can send a note to the whole study, a lead and its researchers, or a
specific researcher. The next eligible model request receives the note and
records its ID on the receipt. The reservation and steering controls share a
database lock so an admitted request keeps its original input.

Once a lead or researcher saves a finding, the owner can send the confirmed
findings to the editor. The swarm settles requests already admitted and stops
admitting additional investigations. The editor receives the available findings
and the list of unfinished work. Pending expert questions remain unanswered
unless the owner recorded an answer. The existing workflow, evidence, and approval
of the working sentence remain pinned.

## Continuing a failed study

An explicit resume can restart an eligible provider failure on the same study.
Completed receipts, prior decisions, and artifacts remain available. A failed
receipt is retained when a new attempt takes its place. The worker preserves
the uncertainty fence for provider outcomes it could have submitted without
recording the result. New provider errors retain sanitized diagnostic fields
when the provider supplied them.

## Expert checkpoints in new Desk studies

On 2026-11-26 the owner asked to exercise judgment more often while research can
still change. The Desk now pins a consultation policy alongside the lead limits.
With **Each lead checks in**, a lead can ask immediately or delegate one batch
and bring the findings back. It needs its own recorded answer before delegating
again or finishing. A bounded lead reserves its final turn for synthesis.
The owner can still explicitly send confirmed findings to the editor early.

The existing consultation identity and journal carry the answer on replay. Older
swarm configurations omit the new field and retain their discretionary behavior.
New Desk studies also default to a separate editorial review. Both settings can
be changed before launch. [Expert checkpoint and workspace delivery](https://github.com/andersj05/ResearchChain/issues/184)
