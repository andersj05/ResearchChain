# Start a study from source material

The owner wants to bring a week of charts, notes and conversations to the
Research desk before having a thesis. The first useful decision is what the
material makes worth investigating. The lead should bring that decision to the
owner. [Interfaces, Brains, and Computers](https://equitycats.substack.com/p/interfaces-brains-and-computers).

## Decision

Offer a source-led starting point alongside the research brief. Pin the
selected source versions. A first pass by the lead reads that packet and
brings back an observation, a question for the owner and up to two provisional
sentences. Each proposal identifies its evidence, a rival reading and the
first investigation it would commission. A thin packet can yield an open
question with no proposed sentence.

Pause before delegation. The owner can take a proposed sentence, rewrite it,
write their own or park the study. Pin the first reading and the owner's
wording separately. Give both to the team when it plans and to the researchers
when they receive their assignments. Show the issued assignments beside the
judgment with a path to their saved requests. Keep the later editorial review
available for decisions about the report.

## Consequences

The first pass costs one lead request when the owner starts a source-led study.
Use the existing evidence receipts, immutable workflow, durable review and
research workspaces. Parking ends that experiment before delegation. A revision
can reopen the packet. Brief-led studies keep their pinned execution path.
